## 🚀 About NanzMusify

**NanzMusify** is a premier open-source Android music streaming client designed from the ground up for true music lovers. It eliminates distractions, respects your privacy, and delivers seamless audio playback powered by YouTube Music InnerTube and advanced synchronized lyrics engines.

---

## ✨ Features

<div align="center">

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <div align="left">
        <h3>🎧 Playback & Streaming</h3>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Zero Ads:</strong> Pure, uninterrupted audio streaming</li>
          <li><strong>Background Playback:</strong> Powered by AndroidX Media3 Service</li>
          <li><strong>Offline Downloads & Caching:</strong> Listen anywhere without internet</li>
          <li><strong>Account Support:</strong> Access personal playlists, liked tracks, and subscriptions</li>
          <li><strong>Local Audio:</strong> Play your local device audio files seamlessly</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <div align="left">
        <h3>🎛️ Audio Engine & DSP</h3>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Loudness Normalization:</strong> EBU R128 standard volume balancing</li>
          <li><strong>Audio Controls:</strong> Tempo, pitch, and playback speed adjustment</li>
          <li><strong>Smooth Crossfade:</strong> Gapless transition between songs</li>
          <li><strong>Equalizer Integration:</strong> Built-in system equalizer & spatial audio</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <div align="left">
        <h3>🎤 Synchronized Lyrics & Discovery</h3>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Live Synced Lyrics:</strong> Multi-source lyrics (LRCLIB, Kugou, SimpMusic, BetterLyrics, Unison)</li>
          <li><strong>Lyrics Translation:</strong> Word-by-word romanization and translation support</li>
          <li><strong>Music Recognition:</strong> Identify songs playing in your surroundings</li>
          <li><strong>Rich Metadata:</strong> Detailed artist bios, discographies, and related albums</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <div align="left">
        <h3>🎨 Modern Material You Interface</h3>
        <ul>
          <li><strong>Material 3 & Dynamic Colors:</strong> UI auto-adapts to album artwork</li>
          <li><strong>Player Canvas & Visuals:</strong> High-resolution album art & canvas backgrounds</li>
          <li><strong>Multiple Player Layouts:</strong> Tailored styles & widgets for your home screen</li>
          <li><strong>Responsive Design:</strong> Optimized for smartphones, foldables, and tablets</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
